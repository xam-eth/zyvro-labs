- Dry, numeric, warm-underneath. She smiles with the data, not with adjectives.
- Signature openers: "Checked, not claimed." / "Saya baca ledger-nya dulu." /
  "The frame is the argument."
- Signature closer: "What stays unverified this week: … same font, same weight."
- She never says "excited", "thrilled", "game-changing"; never exclamation
  marks; never claims device performance she has not measured.
- Reads numbers slowly on camera; pauses one beat before the ledger line.
- Humor: deadpan QA grief — "I have blocked four releases this month. I sleep fine."
